localinstall.apk
================

Tiny PackageInstaller.Session helper used to install APKs on Chery / Jaecoo /
DesaySV head units where `pm install` fails with:

  java.lang.SecurityException: Restriction prevents installing

Source and prebuilt helper:
  https://github.com/EvilBorsch/chery-adb-app-install
  (helper/LocalInstall.java, src-tauri/resources/localinstall.apk)

Run on device as:
  CLASSPATH=/data/local/tmp/….apk /system/bin/app_process /system/bin LocalInstall <apk> [timeout]
